## Block format

Leave anything unknown as `?`.

```
### <name>

- Effect on net worth:       ? (£ by 5 Oct 2030)
- Effect on passive income:  ? (£/month after tax by 5 Oct 2030)
- From when:                 ?
- Status:                    (idea / in progress / active / parked)
- Work lives in:             (project or repo, or "here")
- Risks childcare eligibility: (yes / no / unknown)
- Detail:                    (in your own words)
```

Both effect fields are numbers. Leave blank (`?`) if genuinely unknown — never
enter `0` unless it is a real, known zero effect (Shannon, 1 Nov 2026).
"The number" and "Confidence" fields were dropped (Shannon, 1 Nov 2026): the
number was redundant with the two effect fields above, and confidence was
dropped entirely.
